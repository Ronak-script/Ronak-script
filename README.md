# Ronak

Co-founder of [Clairvyn](https://clairvyn.com).

Clairvyn gives architects a floorplan from their requirements. They describe the project, through a form or in their own words, and get back a plan they can download and keep working on.

I work across the product: floorplan generation, the web app architects use, and the systems that keep it running.

My work is in private Clairvyn repositories, so most of it does not show up here as public code.
